Digital Support & Security

National competition for learners who configure, secure, and troubleshoot real IT systems under pressure.

Express interest Official competition page

What it is

A merged national skills competition for IT support and networks.

Digital Support & Security is crucial to the economy and day-to-day functioning of the modern world. This WorldSkills UK competition focuses on the essentials of a career in digital support and security across industries. It brings together the former IT Support Technician and Network Infrastructure Technician competitions into one pathway.

  • 01
    Client / server networks Set up a domain controller and configure systems to meet customer requirements.
  • 02
    Operating systems & IP Configure OS settings and IP addresses on end devices.
  • 03
    CISCO networking Configure, maintain, and troubleshoot ICT networks, connecting devices to a LAN.

Skills you need

Technical ability plus calm decision-making under time pressure.

Judges look for more than tool knowledge. You need to plan, communicate, and recover when something breaks.

  • IT skills
  • Attention to detail
  • Time management
  • Problem solving
  • Interpersonal skills
  • Troubleshooting
  • Flexibility
  • Planning & organising

Who it’s for

Aimed at Level 3–4 digital / networking pathways.

Competitors will most likely be studying an apprenticeship such as:

  • L3 Digital Support Technician
  • L3 IT Solutions Technician
  • L3 Information Communications Technician
  • L4 Network Engineer

Or working towards qualifications including:

  • HNC / HND Computer Networking
  • HNC / HND Cyber Security

Not sure you fit? Still register your interest below — tutors can advise against the current handbook and entry rules.
